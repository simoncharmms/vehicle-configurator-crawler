    Methodology and source assumptions

    Historical ratios use all imported purchase cohorts, not just the latest day or the displayed 90 available points. Exposures are option-level eligibility counts, not unique buyers or market sales. Inventory is excluded from fitting.

    Unchanged price is the default. A scenario changes the latest reference price once, then holds it constant. Configured sensitivity is a prior assumption, not learned elasticity; fitted sensitivity is an association, not a causal effect. Large price changes are extrapolations with clipped features.

    Intervals use a local Gaussian/Laplace approximation conditional on the selected source, model and priors. Dependence, selection bias, future shocks and misspecification are not fully represented. Synthetic intervals describe the simulation only.

    Catalogue-context history follows the published artifact; on purchase-only dates its price may be the cohort price, not an independently observed catalogue quote. Demand prices are exposure-weighted cohort prices. Missing demand is not a zero take rate.

      Published cohort archive

      Only aggregate, authorized data belongs in public artifacts. A synthetic archive is a mapping template, not observed purchases; replacing its labels does not create real evidence. Cohort archives cover the revision scope, which may differ from cached market endpoints.
